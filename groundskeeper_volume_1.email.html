01  Plant & Sow This Week

This is the fall-garden pivot week for zone 7. The summer beds are still producing, but the smart money is already on autumn.

❧  Direct sow: bush beans, beets, carrots, Swiss chard, turnips, kale, radishes, plus one last round of cucumbers and summer squash.

❧  Start indoors now: fall broccoli, cauliflower, and cabbage for transplanting in late August.

Rule of Thumb
Add about 14 days to the seed packet's "days to maturity" for fall sowings. Shorter days slow everything down.
02  Tasks in the Beds

❧  Water deep, water early. Raised beds dry fast in summer heat. Mulch new fall sowings lightly to keep the soil cool and moist for germination.

❧  Succession sow beans and beets every 1 to 2 weeks rather than all at once.

❧  Pest watch: peak season for squash bugs, cucumber beetles, and tomato hornworms. Check the undersides of squash leaves for egg clusters.

❧  Harvest often: pick zucchini, cukes, and beans every 1 to 2 days to keep plants producing. Pull garlic and onions if the tops have flopped.
