01  Plant & Sow This Week

September opens a dependable sowing window for quick cool-season crops while tomatoes, peppers, and squash finish their summer run.

❧  Sow now: spinach, leaf and romaine lettuce, and salad radishes. Rutgers lists all four for September planting in New Jersey.

❧  Last call outdoors: sow turnip greens, mustard greens, and daikon radishes by September 10; baby lettuce and salad mix can continue through September 15, and salad radishes through September 30.

Rule of Thumb
Sow short rows every 7 to 10 days instead of one large planting. The staggered starts spread harvest and reduce the risk from one hot or dry spell.
02  Tasks in the Beds

❧  Use the dry window. Labor Day and Tuesday look sunny and mild—good conditions for sowing, transplanting, and resetting a tired bed.

❧  Keep seedbeds moist. New lettuce, spinach, and radish rows need steady surface moisture until germination even when established plants can go longer between watering.

❧  Clean as crops finish. Remove spent and diseased plants, keep diseased material out of the compost, and record where each crop grew for next year's rotation.

❧  Plan the soil test. Rutgers recommends fall as a good time to test vegetable-garden soil. Sample before adding fertilizer so the report reflects the bed you actually have.
