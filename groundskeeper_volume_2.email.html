01  Plant & Sow This Week

This is the late-summer handoff week: keep harvesting the heat lovers, but give the open bed space to quick fall crops now.

❧  Sow now: spinach, leaf lettuce, radishes, turnip greens, turnip roots, mustard greens, kale, Swiss chard, and green bunching onions.

❧  Last call: August 10 is Rutgers' South Jersey cutoff for bush beans, beets, cabbage, and cauliflower. Broccoli transplants can continue through August 20; kale transplants through August 30.

Rule of Thumb
For hot-soil crops such as lettuce and spinach, sow in late-day shade, keep the seedbed evenly moist, and use light cover until germination.
02  Tasks in the Beds

❧  Beat Monday's heat. Water deeply before the afternoon reaches the 90s, but do not count forecast thunderstorms as irrigation until you check the soil.

❧  Protect new sowings. Shade cloth or a light board over the row can prevent the surface from baking; remove solid covers as soon as seedlings emerge.

❧  Pest watch: inspect brassicas for imported cabbageworm and squash leaves for eggs; hand-pick early before populations build.

❧  Harvest and reset: pick cucumbers, beans, squash, and tomatoes frequently. Remove spent plants, leave diseased material out of the compost, and top-dress cleared beds before fall planting.
